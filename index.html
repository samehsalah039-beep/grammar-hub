Grammar Topics

Grade 11 Grammar Focus

Each grammar topic is explained in a student-friendly way and reinforced through interactive narrative cloze activities with multiple-choice gaps.

Conditionals

Third Conditional

Used to imagine unreal past situations and their results. Structure: If + past perfect, would have + past participle.

Vocabulary

Phrasal Verbs

Non-separable phrasal verbs stay together. Example: "look after the children" — not "look the children after."

Communication

Reported Speech

Used to report what someone said without using their exact words. Tenses usually shift back.

Passive Voice

Past Perfect Passive

Structure: had been + past participle. Focuses on actions completed before another past action.

Narration

Past Perfect Continuous

Shows an action that continued over a period of time before another event in the past.

Cohesion

Adverbial Linkers

Words such as however, therefore, meanwhile, and consequently connect ideas smoothly.

Past Habits

Used To

Describes repeated actions or states in the past that no longer happen now.
